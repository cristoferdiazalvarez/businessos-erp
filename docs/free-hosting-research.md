# Free hosting compatibility review

Checked against provider documentation on **2026-11-13**. Budget is **EUR 0/month**, with no card, paid subscription, automatic overages or trial-to-paid enrollment. Limits below are not promises of future pricing or availability. Account-specific eligibility is checked before resource creation; if any payment/upgrade screen appears, stop.

## Selected: Vercel Hobby + Neon Free

Use two new Vercel projects and one new Neon project. Next.js remains the frontend, NestJS remains the API, Prisma remains the ORM and PostgreSQL remains the real persistence layer. Production API access is proxied through the web origin for first-party cookies. The owner approved these three free projects, but account connection and hosted verification are still required.

The public site is a personal portfolio with fictional data, no checkout, paid users, ads or real business operations. Vercel's [Hobby terms](https://vercel.com/legal/terms) restrict use to personal/non-commercial purposes. This is not a free hosting recommendation for a commercial ERP service.

CPU/time/storage limits are different measurements and must not be treated as equivalent across providers. Idle resumption does not equal data loss. Neon storage persists while compute sleeps; Supabase's free pause may require owner action; Render's database has an explicit expiry.

## Availability and activation boundary

Free accounts can have anti-abuse checks, geographic restrictions, quota sharing, cold starts and no SLA. No provider account has been proven eligible merely by reading pricing. No automatic paid upgrade, billing Marketplace integration, domain purchase, trial or new paid team is permitted. Never change an existing account's plan or services to make this work.

The three free resources are authorized, but only after actual plan verification and technical checks. The remaining user action is official Vercel/Neon account connection. Hosted build/package validation, real Neon TLS/pooling and public certificate/browser checks follow that connection; they have not been replaced by local simulations. See [deployment procedure](./deployment.md).
